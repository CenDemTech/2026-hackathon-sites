Real-world power-ups
  1. Turn on Global Privacy Control. Some browsers and extensions can send an opt-out signal to every site you visit.
  2. Learn your state's rights. A growing number of states let you access, delete and opt out of the sale of your data.
  3. Tidy your phone settings. Reset your ad ID and share location only while an app is in use.
  4. Speak up. Tell your members of Congress that strong privacy protections should not depend on where you live.
