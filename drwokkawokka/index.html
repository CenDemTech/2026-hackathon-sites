Privacy

Your listening stays yours

The room sees which songs you have in common. It never sees your whole library.

Read-only access

We only ask to read your top tracks and playlists. We can't post, follow or change anything.

Only the overlap is shared

Friends see a count of who knows each song, not who listens to what.

Disconnect anytime

Leave a room or unlink your service and your data is removed. Rooms expire after [RETENTION PERIOD].

No account needed

Rather not link? Paste a public playlist link, or just use the classics.
