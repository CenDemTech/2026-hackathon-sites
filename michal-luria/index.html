Michal Luria is a Senior Research Fellow at the Center for Democracy & Technology, leading its research on AI chatbots and child safety online. Her qualitative, mixed-methods and human-centered research explores and critiques interactions with emerging technologies, and translates what it finds into policy, ethics and advocacy. She holds a Ph.D. in Human-Computer Interaction from Carnegie Mellon University. Her writing has appeared in Wired, CNN and MIT Technology Review; her work has been featured by NBC, BBC News, The Verge and Dezeen.
